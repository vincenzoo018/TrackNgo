1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 Project Context
The Rapid growth of information and communication due to technological advancements. These are influencing the manner in which business and organizations deal with information [1]. Electronic Document Management Systems are critical in organizing document management according to Gamido, Gamido and Macaspac. They facilitate organization, storage and retrieval of documents. In his discussion on the application of technology in governance and its impact on development, Chowdhury indicated that it increases transparency and accountability of governments. Technology ensures efficient delivery of public service through elimination of bottlenecks [2]. Therefore, governments and businesses in the world are utilizing digital means to facilitate easy workflow and access to information in order to make appropriate decisions. In the Philippines, the government is utilizing technology to ensure security of record and ease of information sharing and delivery of services. The Electronic Document Management Systems form part of the efforts.
Based on the information gathered during the Reverse Pitching and interview activity with the DOST Head, DOST personnel, and personnel from the Office of the Mayor of Mati, document processing is currently performed through a manual, paper-based workflow. Documents handled by the office include applications, permits, requests, letters, financial documents, legislative papers, memoranda, endorsements, reports, and communications from other government agencies. These documents are classified into two categories: external documents and internal documents. External documents originate from citizens, clients, private organizations, or other government agencies and are first received by the Receiving Clerk. The Receiving Clerk records the document, assigns a tracking number, prepares a routing slip, and forwards the document to the appropriate department based on its purpose. The routing slip indicates the offices responsible for processing the document and, when necessary, the officials required to review or sign it, such as the Department Head, City Administrator, or the Mayor. However, not all external documents require approval or signatures. Some submissions, such as documentary requirements, requests for verification, or supporting documents, are only checked, recorded, or processed by the responsible office before being filed or released without passing through the complete approval workflow. Internal documents originate from offices or departments within the City Government, such as the City Planning Office, Human Resource Office, Accounting Office, or external government partners like DOST during official coordination. These documents are manually forwarded directly to the intended office or approving authority, depending on the nature of the transaction. They may be routed to the Mayor, the City Administrator, another department, or other authorized officials for review, endorsement, or approval. After all required actions have been completed, documents intended for clients are released by the responsible office, while documents that serve as official records remain in the department where the transaction is completed and are manually filed and archived for future reference. This manual document routing, approval, release, and archiving process remains the common practice in many local government units in the Philippines, where document movement is primarily monitored through paper records and routing slips rather than an automated document management system [3].
The current manual document processing in the Office of the Mayor of Mati makes it difficult for personnel and clients to monitor the status and movement of documents throughout the workflow. Since routing depends on paper-based records and manual coordination among departments, document updates often require repeated follow-ups because the clients do not know the status of the submitted documents. These challenges are evident in the related literature that has limited procedures and policies in managing document [4]. Effective workflow tracking enables organizations to monitor document movement, improve process visibility, and identify bottlenecks that affect the timely completion of transactions [10]. In addition, digital signature integration enhances the authenticity and integrity of documents by providing secure electronic approval and verification throughout the document lifecycle [11]. As presented in Table 1, although existing document management systems offer basic document storage and monitoring capabilities, they do not collectively provide QR code-based tracking, SMS notifications, smart escalation for ARTA-compliant processing, and password-protected document export. These limitations demonstrate the need for a more comprehensive document management and monitoring system that improves workflow visibility, communication, document security, and service efficiency in the Office of the Mayor of Mati.

1.2. Purpose and Description
TrackNGo is a web-responsive document management and monitoring system developed for the Office of the Mayor of Mati to improve the efficiency, security, transparency, and accountability of document processing. The system digitizes document routing through a centralized workflow, enabling users to submit, route, review, approve, monitor, and archive documents electronically. It supports the Mayor, Department Heads, Executive Assistants and Administrators, Records Officers and Receiving Clerks, and CART Personnel by streamlining document handling and promoting compliance with government processing timelines.
To enhance document management, TrackNGo integrates Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to convert scanned documents into searchable digital records [15], QR code-based document tracking for real-time monitoring, digital signatures for secure approvals, SMS notifications for timely updates, and an EODB-Compliant Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm, QR code-based document tracking for real-time monitoring, digital signatures for secure approvals, SMS notifications for timely updates, and an EODB-Compliant Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm to improve accountability and support compliance with Republic Act No. 11032. The development of TrackNGo also supports Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 9, which promotes resilient infrastructure, innovation, and the adoption of technology to improve public services and foster sustainable development [12].
1.3. Objectives
1.3.1 General Objectives
The aim of this study is to develop a web responsive document management and monitoring system under the Laravel framework and MySQL. This system introduces processes that will be advantageous to the citizens of the City of Mati such as workflow tracking, QR code and SMS integration for document tracking, digital document integration, Smart Escalation feature and Password-Gated Document to enhance the accountability, transparency, security and accessibility of their overall document and monitoring process.

1.4. Scope and Limitations
This study describes the development and implementation of TrackNGo, a web-responsive Document Tracking and Management System for the Office of the Mayor of Mati, Davao Oriental. The system is developed using the Laravel Framework and provides a centralized platform for managing documents throughout their lifecycle, from submission to archiving. Its core modules include centralized document management for uploading, storing, organizing, and retrieving files; an FSM-Based Workflow Routing and Document Lifecycle module; real-time document tracking; QR code-based document monitoring; digital signature authentication; automated SMS notifications; Smart Escalation for monitoring document processing timelines; password-gated document export; Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for digitizing and extracting searchable text from scanned physical documents; and monitoring and reporting dashboards. The OCR module assists receiving personnel by automatically extracting printed text from scanned documents, reducing manual data entry, improving document indexing, and enabling full-text search across the document repository [15]. The system is intended to be used by authorized personnel, including the City Mayor, Department Heads, Executive Assistants and Administrators, Records Officers and Receiving Clerks, CART Personnel (Oversight and Compliance Officers), and external clients who need to monitor the status of their submitted documents. The deployment, implementation, and evaluation of the system are limited to the Office of the Mayor of Mati and its administrative jurisdiction. Furthermore, the system is designed to manage official government records such as requests, endorsements, permits, memoranda, reports, correspondence, and transaction logs generated from the date of deployment onward.
Despite its capabilities, the study has several operational limitations and boundaries. TrackNGo is a web-based application that requires a stable internet connection for users to access the system, monitor document status, perform workflow transactions, and receive real-time updates. Although the system supports digital signatures for document approval and authentication, these signatures are intended for internal organizational use and do not replace legally recognized digital certificates issued by accredited certification authorities unless integrated with such external services. Likewise, the Smart Escalation feature operates based on predefined workflow rules, processing timelines, and configurable parameters established by authorized system administrators in accordance with organizational policies and applicable regulations. The OCR functionality is intended only for printed documents with readable text and may produce lower recognition accuracy when processing handwritten documents, damaged files, or low-quality scanned images. The system evaluation is limited to authorized users within the Office of the Mayor of Mati, including employees and administrators who directly utilize the application in their daily operations. Consequently, the evaluation results reflect the experiences and operational requirements of the target organization and may not be directly generalizable to other local government units or institutions with different document management procedures, organizational structures, or workflow requirements. As such, TrackNGo is specifically developed to address the document management, monitoring, and compliance needs of the Office of the Mayor of Mati.

The Proponents are going to collect data from the Office of the Mayor of Mati. They want to learn how electronic workflows work. They will go through papers like routing slips and records and documents processed by the office, including requests, endorsements, permits, memoranda and reports. The proponents want to see the papers shuffling around the office. They will talk to office staff, like clerks and supervisors to learn how long things take to process. The information will be used by the proponents to build a model of the workflows. They will focus on the Office of the Mayor of Mati and its daily work. Through the data gathering process, they will be able to know the office's needs. The study is to improve the office's workflows. They will review the data they gather to determine the areas for improvement. And the Office of the Mayor of Mati will be the beneficiary of this study.
2.2. Analysis
The Analysis phase focuses on examining the information gathered during the Reverse Pitching to identify the functional and non-functional requirements of the proposed TrackNGo system. During this phase, the proponents analyzed the existing document management procedures, identified workflow bottlenecks, evaluated user responsibilities, and determined the technological resources required to support the system. The results of the analysis served as the basis for defining the system's user requirements, hardware and software requirements, functional and non-functional requirements, and feasibility requirements. Through this analysis, the proponents ensured that the proposed system addresses the operational needs of the Office of the Mayor of Mati while supporting efficient, secure, and transparent document management. The proposed system does not replace or alter the existing document processing procedures of the Office of the Mayor of Mati; instead, it digitizes and automates the current manual workflow while preserving the established policies, approval hierarchy, and operational practices, allowing personnel to continue following existing procedures with the support of a centralized digital system.

2.2.1.6 To allow authorized users to generate reports, view audit trails, and securely export documents through password-protected document export.

2.2.3. Functional and Non Functional Requirement
2.2.3.1 Functional  Requirements
2.2.3.1.1 Login Page - The Login page is to serve as the access point of the system wherein, every member/users who register to the system and gets to log-in via username, and password then be able to gain entry to the said system that automatically redirect the member to the respective areas assigned to his role with module to access that is permitted, to him/her, to follow and adhere as a member of Office of the Mayor of Mati. 
2.2.3.1.2 User Management Module - This module allows the Executive Administrator to be able to insert a new user, modify details of a user, to delete an existing user or to block/deactivate a user as well to insert new departments, edit/modify departments, insert new categories and edit/modify of categories. He/She must be able to define the setting of the system to manage all available functions of a document management process and to be aware of who a user would fall in one position in the office.
2.2.3.1.3 Document Registration and Management Module - The module that would serve as responsible in registering, cataloguing, storing, sorting, withdrawing, returning, withdrawing, updating and archiving of any and all documents which may be involved in the database or repository. During the act of registering an original document; a unique system reference number, a tracking number as well as QR code will be automatic. Original documents' detail will be accompanied by the new records along their lifecycle.
2.2.3.1.4 OCR Document Recognition Module- During document registration with automatic document registration and automatic scanning of documents using OCR This system recognizes scanned images with OCR and extracts printed information and converted to storable data in the database, and displays extracted information in the temporary view for verification of a receiving department and is stored in the database. Also supports automatic metadata generation, automatic indexing of keywords, and document classification.  
2.2.3.1.5 Document Routing  Page - This document would serve as responsible to hand over/ deliver documents to another office with an automatic system generated electronic routing slip which contained original document details, sender and receiver's information, designated receiving office, instruction and status. The user is to send a document, forward document, to reject document, to return document, or to endorse the said original document.
2.2.3.1.6 Document Workflow Management Module - This module handles documents through the Finite State Machine (FSM)-Based Document Lifecycle Management (DLCM). In FSM-Based DLCM, states define possible documents' conditions while transitions represent documents' moves in the entire lifecycle of any document. The FSM-Based DLCM restricts documents on an invalid move from one state to another.
2.2.3.1.7 Document Approval Page - An interface for the approved individuals, in any status the document reaches, to be able to electronically sign, approve, reject or return documents according to the defined workflow. The screen would show all approvals made by the individual officials with their e-signatures. 
2.2.3.1.8 Document Monitoring Dashboard - Screen to provide a quick and summary look on how the documents are at any given time including the current physical location, assigned office, in which stage, pending documents from an office or an official, which document is late with a defined time-frame and a listing of the approved documents.
2.2.3.1.9 Notification and Smart Escalation Module - This module sends SMS to the sender as well as the relevant individuals as designated when status is changed. Moreover, it continuously monitors if any document surpassed the set timeline based on Republic Act 11032 and sends notification when there is an escalation or delay. 
2.2.3.1.10 Audit Trail Module - This Module which would keep logs of every activity that happened in the system including user actions, status changes of any document, timestamps, document details, workflow moves and file export details for all of the transactions and for the document export transactions.
2.2.3.1.11 Reports Dashboard Page - This page presents various reports developed from different modules of the system, providing a single view for monitoring document transactions, workflow processes, office performance, processing status, pending items, escalation history and so on. The dashboard allows the designated user to overlook the entire process, analyze system efficiency and examine office performance so as to facilitate management decisions and ensure procedure conformity.

Figure 2 illustrates the Input-Process-Output (IPO) conceptual framework of the proposed TrackNGo system. The framework shows how user information, documents, and transaction data are collected as inputs, processed 
through the system's document management and workflow modules, and transformed into real-time monitoring dashboards, reports, notifications, and securely processed documents. It demonstrates the overall flow of data from document submission to final output.
2.3.1.1 Input - This phase represents the data required to initiate document processing in the TrackNGo system. It includes user information, system configurations, uploaded documents, and transaction details entered by authorized personnel and clients. These inputs are validated and stored in the database before being forwarded to the workflow process, ensuring that complete and accurate information is available for document management.
2.3.1.2 Process - This phase represents the core operations of the TrackNGo system. Documents are registered, digitized using Optical Character Recognition (OCR), assigned QR codes, and routed through an FSM-Based Document Lifecycle Management Model. Throughout the workflow, the system manages document tracking, digital signatures, SMS notifications, password-protected document export, and the EODB-Compliant Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm to monitor processing timelines and automatically notify designated personnel of overdue transactions.
2.3.1.3 Output -This phase represents the information and services produced after document processing. The system generates real-time dashboards, monitoring reports, audit trails, SMS notifications, and QR code-verified documents with digital signatures. These outputs improve document traceability, transparency, accountability, and communication while supporting efficient document management in the Office of the Mayor of Mati.

2.3.2.  EODB-Compliant Smart Escalation Algorithm

The EODB-Compliant Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm is the primary innovation and capstone element of the proposed TrackNGo system. Within the conceptual framework presented in Figure 3, the algorithm is implemented as one of the major components of the Process phase together with the Finite State Machine (FSM)-Based Document Workflow, QR Code Generation, Digital Signature Processing, and SMS Gateway Integration. While the FSM governs valid document transitions throughout the workflow lifecycle, the Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm continuously monitors the processing time of every active document to ensure compliance with the service delivery standards prescribed under Republic Act No. 11032, otherwise known as the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act [7].
The processing pipeline begins immediately after user information, uploaded documents, organizational settings, and transaction details are collected during the Input phase of the system. These inputs pass through the data ingestion layer before being processed by the Laravel application. Documents are then registered, assigned to the appropriate office, and routed through the FSM workflow. During document processing, the Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm continuously computes the elapsed working days of each active document and compares them with the corresponding ARTA processing period of 3, 7, or 20 working days. If the processing period remains within the allowable limit, the FSM continues routing the document through the normal workflow states of Submission, Review, Approval, Forwarding, Return, Rejection, and Completion. However, once the prescribed processing period has been exceeded, the algorithm automatically interrupts the normal workflow by locking further processing, recording the escalation event, updating the document status to Escalated, notifying responsible personnel through the integrated SMS Gateway, and automatically rerouting the document to the Office of the Mayor for immediate administrative action.
The algorithm also integrates with the Audit Trail module by permanently recording every escalation event, notification, workflow transition, and administrative action performed after escalation. Consequently, the proposed approach extends beyond traditional document management and tracking systems by actively enforcing compliance with Republic Act No. 11032 instead of merely recording document status. Unlike the systems developed by Jaca [4], Bermeo et al. [5], and Acedo et al. [6], which primarily focus on document routing, monitoring, and tracking, the proposed TrackNGo system automatically enforces processing deadlines through workflow interruption, document locking, intelligent escalation, and automatic rerouting to higher management whenever processing periods are violated.
As illustrated in the IPO conceptual framework, the outputs generated from this processing pipeline include updated workflow dashboards, administrative reports, downloadable transaction histories, automated SMS notifications, QR-code verified documents, and audit trail records that collectively improve transparency, accountability, and regulatory compliance. Through this integrated processing mechanism, the Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm directly addresses the problems identified in Section 1.1, including prolonged document processing, limited workflow visibility, lack of accountability for delayed transactions, and the absence of automated mechanisms for enforcing EODB and ARTA processing standards [7].
Figure 3 illustrates that the Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm forms an integral component of the processing pipeline of the proposed TrackNGo system. After user information, uploaded documents, organizational settings, workflow requests, and transaction details are collected during the Input phase, the information passes through the system's Data Ingestion Layer, where it is validated before entering the Laravel application. The application then performs the necessary operations and forwards each document to the Finite State Machine (FSM)-Based Document Workflow, which governs all valid workflow state transitions.
While the FSM controls document movement throughout the workflow, the Smart Escalation Routing Algorithm executes simultaneously as a background monitoring service. It continuously retrieves active documents from the Documents table together with their assigned processing dates and corresponding ARTA processing thresholds of 3, 7, or 20 working days, as prescribed under Republic Act No. 11032 [7]. This continuous workflow monitoring enables organizations to monitor document processing performance, identify delays, and improve compliance with government service delivery standards through automated process monitoring and intervention [8].

After determining that a document has exceeded its allowable processing period, the algorithm computes the number of overdue working days and assigns the corresponding escalation severity level. This approach classifies delays according to the extent by which the document exceeded its prescribed processing period rather than the original ARTA processing category. Consequently, the escalation mechanism is applied consistently to all document types regardless of whether their allowable processing period is 3, 7, or 20 working days.
Once the escalation level has been determined, the algorithm automatically executes a sequence of system-controlled operations. The affected document is immediately locked to prevent further processing or modification by the office responsible for the delay, thereby preserving the integrity of the document during the escalation process. The document status is then updated to Escalated, and the complete escalation details including the allowable processing period, elapsed working days, overdue working days, escalation level, notification status, and timestamp are recorded in the ARTA_Escalations table.
After recording the escalation event, the integrated SMS Gateway automatically sends notifications to the designated oversight personnel regarding the delayed transaction. The system subsequently reroutes the document to the Office of the Mayor for administrative review and appropriate action. Throughout the escalation process, every workflow transition, notification, routing activity, status update, and administrative action is permanently recorded in the Audit Trail module to ensure transparency, accountability, and traceability of all system activities.
Upon completion of the Smart Escalation process, the updated document status becomes immediately available through the system dashboards and monitoring reports. The generated outputs including escalation notifications, administrative reports, audit logs, updated workflow dashboards, and document status reports provide decision-makers with real-time information for monitoring document processing performance and ensuring compliance with the service delivery periods mandated by Republic Act No. 11032.

The use case diagram represents the relationship of functions between the various users of the system and the TrackNGo Document Tracking and Management System. It also showcases  the different stakeholders for the system and their respective roles and responsibilities throughout the process of handling documents. 2.3.5 Architecture Design
    
 
Figure 6. TrackNGo System Architecture   


This section describes the proposed architecture of the TrackNGo system. The proposed architecture follows the client-server paradigm, wherein clients communicate with the server through HTTPS using web applications on their desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. Figure 6 depicts the overall architecture, which consists of a Presentation Layer, an Application Layer, a Database Layer, and External Services.
The Presentation Layer is the client side, used by internal users (Mayor, Department Heads, Executive Administrators, Records Officers, CART Personnel) and external users (clients and requesting parties). It provides a Web Dashboard for document registration and monitoring, and a Mobile and QR Portal for QR scanning and SMS alerts. 
The Application Layer is the server side, built on the Laravel PHP Framework [12]. Nginx handles HTTPS requests, backed by the Laravel server, which runs the FSM-based workflow engine defining document states and transitions. Core modules include Authentication and RBAC (access security), the FSM Workflow Engine (state transitions), OCR Engine (document digitization), Digital Signature (approval authentication), Smart Escalation (ARTA deadline monitoring), and Notification Service (real-time alerts). The Audit Trail logs all workflow actions, while the SMS Gateway and QR Verification module handles external API-based communication and code validation. 
The Database Layer uses MySQL (via XAMPP/phpMyAdmin), storing Core Records (Users, Roles, Departments, Documents, Document_Types) and Workflow and Compliance records (Digital_Signatures, Audit_Trail, ARTA_Escalations, Routing_Slips, Report_Logs), with a separate File Share for scanned attachments and signed files.
The Notification Service also connects to External Services via API, including the SMS Gateway, QR Code generator/validator, and digital signature API.Recent studies support the core technologies behind this design. Authentication and access-control governance frameworks reinforce the importance of TrackNGo's RBAC module for securing user access [16]. OCR-based digitization research shows that scanned document pipelines can reliably convert legacy records into searchable digital formats, supporting the OCR Engine [17]. Studies on real-time alert systems show that layered architectures with multi-channel notification (mobile, SMS, dashboard) achieve low-latency, reliable delivery, aligning with TrackNGo's Smart Escalation and Notification Service [18].
